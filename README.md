# Wakepoint

Top-down space shooter. Hold the orbit, cut through enemy waves, and grab power-ups before the signal dies.

## Play

- **WASD / arrows** — move
- **Mouse / pointer** — aim
- **Click, hold, or Space** — fire
- Hold the pointer with no keys to fly toward your aim
- Touch: left stick moves, right side aims and fires
- **Esc / P** — pause · **M** — mute

## Features

- Escalating waves (drones, fighters, bruisers)
- Power-ups: tri-shot, shield, burn speed, extra life
- Lives, score, and a local high-score board
- Parallax starfield, muzzle flashes, explosions
- Sign-in (Google / X) to prefills your board name
